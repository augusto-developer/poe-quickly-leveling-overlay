Where to get these gems

A quest only counts once you have talked to the NPC in town after doing it. Only one gem per quest is free; the others are bought from the vendor of that town.

Gems of other classes: Siosa, in The Library (Act 3), sells them after A Fixture of Fate, and Lilly Roth, in Lioneye's Watch (Act 6), sells almost every gem after Fallen from Grace.
